=== BUOC 4 — PHAT HIEN THEM: khuech dai do block event loop ===

Client bao 13.3s/request nhung server chi ghi 2.65s. Chenh lech ~5 lan,
dung bang concurrency=5.

Nguyen nhan: /chat khai bao la "async def" nhung agent.run() la ham dong bo
va goi time.sleep(). time.sleep() trong coroutine se BLOCK event loop,
nen 5 request dong thoi bi xep hang noi tiep thay vi chay song song:
  5 x 2.65s = 13.3s.

Hau qua: mot su co RAG cham 2.5s bien thanh 13.3s duoi tai dong thoi.
Day la loi khuech dai, khong phai nguyen nhan goc, nhung lam su co
nghiem trong hon nhieu lan.
